The modelled region, and where its air comes from

Reading the instrument.

Delhi's worst air is not made in Delhi, so the domain runs from Alwar to Karnal.

Why the domain is this big

Through October and November, smoke from paddy stubble burning in Punjab and Haryana travels 200 to 400 km on the north-westerly flow and arrives over a city that often cannot ventilate. A city-only view cannot show that mechanism at all, so the sources are drawn where they actually are.

Domain and active fire detections

Why two resolutions

Delhi at 2.8 km, the wider NCR at 11 km. A uniform fine grid would be roughly 11,000 cells, most of them farmland.

The honest resolution is coarser than the grid

Meteorology arrives at about 11 km and the chemistry prior at about 40 km, so predicted fields are smoothed to 6 km. That removes structure the model cannot justify rather than inventing structure it does not have.

Vertical structure over time

Reading the instrument.

An inversion is air that gets warmer with height. It puts a lid on the city.

Why a lid matters

Whatever is emitted underneath an inversion cannot escape upward, so concentration climbs even when emissions are flat. Everything else in this system reports a surface number; this is the one view that draws the lid itself, in time and height.

Why three charts and not one

Inversion strength is in kelvin, mixing depth in metres, ventilation coefficient in m²·s⁻¹. Plotting them on one pair of axes needs two or three y-scales, which lets whoever draws the chart choose the story by choosing the scaling. Three panels sharing one x-axis compare honestly.

The two-way coupling

Reading the instrument.

Conventional forecasts treat weather as an input only. The real atmosphere runs a loop, and this solves it.

What the loop is

Aerosol blocks sunlight, the surface heats less, the mixed layer grows shallower, the wind slackens, and the same emissions end up more concentrated, which blocks more sunlight. Because the last step feeds the first it is solved to convergence rather than evaluated once, against a pristine-atmosphere control.

Photolysis, the stronger pathway

aerosol blocks ultraviolet, so less ozone is made
Why this pathway and not the other

There are two aerosol mechanisms and they are not equal. Dimming the sun to cool the surface and shallow the mixed layer changes ozone by 1 to 3 percent. Blocking the ultraviolet that drives the photochemistry changes it by 10 to 12 percent. The second is the one that matters, and it acts on ozone directly.

Why these bounds

They come from the regime the published figures were measured in: winter haze episodes in daylight. A season-wide average, which includes clean afternoons and nights when the radiative feedback is correctly zero, is a different quantity and is reported separately rather than judged.

Stubble plume transport

Reading the instrument.

Every fire detection is released as a parcel and carried on the forecast wind.

Why counting fires is not enough

A detection contributes to Delhi's air only when and where it actually arrives, and only if the lid lets it reach the ground. Smoke can sit overhead while monitors read clean, then land abruptly mid-morning when convection reconnects it to the surface.
