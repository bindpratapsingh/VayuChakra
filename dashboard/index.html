The modelled region, and where its air comes from

Delhi's worst air is not made in Delhi. Through October and November, smoke from paddy stubble burning in Punjab and Haryana travels 200 to 400 km on the north-westerly flow and arrives over a city that often cannot ventilate. A city-only view cannot show that, so the domain runs from Alwar to Karnal and the fire sources are drawn where they actually are.

Why two resolutions

The forecast is consumed in Delhi, so the city is modelled at about 2.8 km. The rest of the NCR only has to carry a plume toward it, and 11 km is enough for that. A uniform fine grid over the whole box would be roughly 11,000 cells, most of them farmland.

The honest resolution is coarser than the display grid. Meteorology arrives at about 11 km and the chemistry prior at about 40 km, so predicted fields are smoothed to 6 km. That removes structure the model cannot justify rather than inventing structure it does not have.

A temperature inversion is air that gets warmer with height, and it puts a lid on the city: whatever is emitted underneath cannot escape upward, so concentration climbs even when emissions are flat. Everything else in this system reports a surface number. Here the lid itself is drawn, in time and height.

Why three charts and not one

Inversion strength is in kelvin, mixing depth in metres, ventilation coefficient in m²·s⁻¹. Plotting them on one pair of axes needs two or three y-scales, which lets whoever draws the chart choose the story by choosing the scaling. Three panels sharing one x-axis compare honestly.

The two-way coupling

Every conventional forecast treats weather as an input only. The real atmosphere runs a loop: aerosol blocks sunlight, the surface heats less, the mixed layer grows shallower, the wind slackens, and the same emissions end up more concentrated, which blocks more sunlight. That loop is what this product is named after, and it is solved to convergence against a pristine-atmosphere control.

Photolysis, the stronger pathway

aerosol blocks ultraviolet, so less ozone is made

There are two aerosol mechanisms and they are not equal. Dimming the sun to cool the surface and shallow the mixed layer changes ozone by 1 to 3 percent. Blocking the ultraviolet that drives the photochemistry changes it by 10 to 12 percent. The second is the one that matters, and it acts on ozone directly.

Bounds come from the regime the published figures were measured in: winter haze episodes in daylight. A season-wide average, which includes clean afternoons and nights when the radiative feedback is correctly zero, is a different quantity and is reported separately rather than judged.

Stubble plume transport

Each satellite fire detection is released as a parcel and carried forward on the forecast wind, spreading as it travels. It contributes to Delhi's air only when and where it actually arrives, and only if the inversion lid lets it reach the ground. Smoke can sit overhead while monitors read clean, then land abruptly mid-morning when convection reconnects it.
