01

The problem statement

Ministry of Earth Sciences, through NCMRWF: an air pollution and weather coupled forecasting system, focused on Delhi NCR.

Delhi's air quality is not a chemistry problem sitting on top of a weather problem. The two are one problem, and the coupling runs in both directions.

Weather sets the volume that pollution occupies. A winter night puts a temperature inversion over the city, the mixed layer collapses to a few hundred metres, and the same emissions land in a fraction of the air. That direction is well understood, and most operational systems model it.

The return path is the harder one. Aerosol is not a passive tracer. It absorbs and scatters incoming sunlight, so the surface heats less, so the boundary layer grows more slowly, so the wind through it slackens, so the pollution concentrates further, so more sunlight is blocked. Separately, the same aerosol removes the ultraviolet that drives photochemistry, which suppresses ozone production directly. A forecast that treats meteorology purely as an input cannot represent either path, and will systematically under-predict exactly the episodes that matter.

On top of that, Delhi's worst air is frequently not made in Delhi. Through October and November, paddy stubble smoke from Punjab and Haryana travels 200 to 400 km on the north westerly flow and arrives over a city that often cannot ventilate. A city sized model cannot see the source at all.

02

What that requires

Nine requirements the statement implies, with the status of each. R1 to R9 are our reading of the statement, not a numbering the statement itself supplies.

R1Two way coupling, solved rather than evaluated Aerosol must feed back onto radiation, temperature, boundary layer and wind, and those must feed back onto concentration. Because the last step feeds the first, it has to be iterated to convergence. Built
R2Numerical weather prediction driving the chemistry Forecast meteorology, not climatology, at the resolution the chemistry is solved on. Partial
R3Resolution useful inside the city A single Delhi number is not a decision support product. Intra city gradients have to survive. Built
R4Multiple pollutants, and a real index PM2.5, PM10, NO2 and O3, combined into the CPCB National AQI by the published breakpoint table rather than predicted as an index directly. Built
R5Long range transport of stubble smoke The fires sit hundreds of kilometres outside the city. Their smoke has to be transported, dispersed, and delivered to the surface on the forecast wind. Built
R6Temperature, wind and PBL height all inside the loop Not only temperature. The statement names all three meteorological responses. Built
R7A 72 hour horizon Useful lead time for a GRAP decision, not a nowcast. Built
R8Validation against observations, including failures Held out in time, held out in space, calibrated uncertainty, and negative results reported as negative. Built
R9Decision support, not just a number GRAP stages trigger at AQI 200, 300 and 400. What a decision maker needs is the probability of crossing one. Built

R2 is marked partial deliberately, and stays partial by construction. We consume a coupled NWP product and apply our own feedback on top of it; we do not run WRF-Chem. Section 04 states exactly where that line sits.

03

What is built, and what it measures

Every figure below was read out of a result file in the repository. None of them are estimates, and none are typed by hand into the interface.

Forecast skill, held out in time

Twelve heads: four pollutants at 24, 48 and 72 hours. Trained on 571,037 station hours across 44 stations, with the winter of 1 Nov 2025 to 28 Feb 2026 removed from training entirely and used as the test set. The baseline is persistence, meaning "tomorrow looks like today", which is unglamorous and genuinely hard to beat at 24 hours. Every head beats it.

Skill where there is no monitor

The forecast serves 1,120 cells and about forty of them contain an instrument. So the question that actually matters is whether it works in a cell it has never seen. Leave one station out removes a station from training entirely and then predicts it, while persistence is still allowed that station's own history.

The coupled loop, and whether its magnitudes are real

The solver runs a damped fixed point iteration over five responses, every one clipped to a physical range, with any cell that will not converge falling back to the uncoupled answer and being counted. On the shipped run it converged in 4 iterations, zero cells fell back, and the maximum residual was 0.46 µg/m³.

The magnitudes are then checked against published Delhi and North India values the model was never fitted to. The check runs in the regime those figures were measured in, meaning high aerosol daylight hours, which is 75,383 cell hours of this run.

The ozone pathway, which is the stronger of the two

The two aerosol mechanisms are not equal. Dimming the sun to cool the surface and shallow the mixed layer moves ozone by 1 to 3 percent. Blocking the ultraviolet that drives the photochemistry moves it by 10 to 12 percent. We model the second, using the MCM v3.3.1 clear sky photolysis parameterisation attenuated by aerosol optical depth.

Stubble transport, calibrated against an operational reference

Every satellite fire detection is released as a parcel and carried forward on the forecast wind, spreading as it travels, contributing to Delhi's surface air only where and when it arrives, and only if the inversion lid lets it reach the ground. Three physics variants were scored over 54 days of October and November 2021 against the MoES and IITM WRF-Chem Decision Support System daily stubble attribution.

Correlation is the column that decides, because only a single multiplicative scale factor is fitted and scaling cannot improve a correlation. So this ranks the physics, not the magnitude. Values in µg/m³. The DSS is third party research output, cited and never redistributed.

Uncertainty a GRAP decision can be made on

A point forecast of 88 µg/m³ against a threshold of 90 says almost nothing useful. Quantile heads at the 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles give the probability of crossing each GRAP trigger instead.

  • The nominal 80% interval covers 75.6% of observations on the held out winter, which the calibration check scores as well calibrated.
  • Quantile crossing rate 0.67%, so the predicted distribution is almost always properly ordered.
  • Brier skill score against climatology for the GRAP II trigger: +0.343.

The grid, and the resolution actually justified

1,120 cells in two tiers: Delhi at 0.025° (about 2.8 km) inside a wider NCR domain at 0.10° (about 11 km), running from Alwar to Karnal so the stubble sources are on the map rather than off it. A uniform fine grid over the whole box would be roughly 11,000 cells, most of them farmland.

The honest resolution is coarser than the display grid

Meteorology arrives at about 11 km and the chemistry prior at about 40 km, so predicted fields are smoothed to 6 km. That removes structure the model cannot justify rather than inventing structure it does not have.

04

What remains, and what will never be there

Two categories: work that is genuinely outstanding, and boundaries chosen on purpose. Stated here so nothing above is read as more than it is.

The coupling ablation has not been scored Outstanding

The most important open question about the coupling is whether it lowers forecast error, not only whether its magnitudes are physically plausible. The ablation panel is built and sits in the repository, but the last full pipeline run skipped the scoring step, so no ablation number ships. An earlier configuration measured −0.11%, which is flat. It should be re-measured on the current multi winter models and reported whichever way it lands.

The hosted instance serves a snapshot, not a live run Outstanding

The free hosting tier allows 512 MB. Measured, the live pipeline peaks at about 1,083 MB for 420 cells and 701 MB for 182 cells, which implies roughly 283 MB of fixed cost before a single cell is forecast. It does not fit at any resolution, so coarsening the domain would have cost real resolution and bought nothing. The deployment therefore serves a precomputed full resolution bundle: it gives up freshness, not resolution and not physics. A paid instance, or a scheduled worker that writes the bundle, would close this.

The intended photolysis validation was ruled out, and is recorded as ruled out Outstanding

The plan was to check modelled ultraviolet attenuation against a measured one, using uv_index over uv_index_clear_sky. Three measurements killed it. Clear sky there means cloud free, not aerosol free, so under 5% cloud the ratio is 0.998 while mean AOD over the same hours is 0.41. The ratio tracks cloud rather than aerosol. And the broadband radiation carries a climatological aerosol, reading 750, 761 and 762 W/m² across rising AOD bands at fixed sun angle. No product in that feed can validate aerosol optics. The ozone response against station observations is used instead, which is a stronger test, but a direct optical validation is still missing.

The comparison against the operational DSS is not like for like Outstanding

Over Oct 2021 to Feb 2022 at 24 hours, our RMSE is 64.26 against the DSS at 98.78 and persistence at 93.98 on the same hours. That is not a fair comparison of forecast skill and must never be presented as one: the DSS was issued operationally and had to predict the weather as well as the chemistry, days ahead, while our hindcast is driven by ERA5 reanalysis, meaning the weather as it actually turned out. What the table supports is that the statistical layer maps meteorology to PM2.5 competitively. A genuine comparison needs our system re-run on archived forecast meteorology.

It is not WRF-Chem, and is not trying to be By design

There is no radiative transfer solve, no chemical mechanism integrated forward in time, and no gridded emissions inventory. This is a coupled surrogate: a statistical forecast whose meteorological inputs are modified by an explicit, iterated, physically bounded feedback model. Building the real thing is a national modelling centre's programme.

No VOC chemistry By design

Delhi's winter ozone is VOC limited as well as radiation limited, and we have no VOC measurements. TROPOMI formaldehyde and NO2 could give a regime map, but that needs orbit level NetCDF processing. The regime itself is established in the literature we cite; only the radiation limited half is modelled.

A single chemical layer By design

Vertical structure is diagnosed, not resolved. The inversion lid and mixing depth are tracked in time and height and drawn, and they modulate the surface concentration, but the chemistry is solved in one layer. This is the largest remaining simplification in the system.

The plume magnitude is partly a DSS emulator By design

One multiplicative scale factor is fitted against the DSS attribution, absorbing emission factor uncertainty (5 to 8 g/kg for cereal straw), satellite detection limits, and burn duration assumptions. The physics parameters are not fitted. So the correlation is an honest result and the absolute magnitude is a calibration, and the two are reported as different things.
